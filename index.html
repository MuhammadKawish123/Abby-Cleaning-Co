Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find yours? Message us on Instagram or use the form above.

Which areas do you serve?

We clean homes and businesses across Toronto and the wider GTA.

Can I book a same-day clean?

Yes, same-day booking is available when there’s space in the schedule. Send your request early in the day.

Are you insured?

Yes. Abby Cleaning Co. is fully insured for your peace of mind.

What is steam cleaning, and where is it used?

Steam cleaning uses hot vapour to loosen dirt from carpets, sofas, bathrooms and kitchens. It’s a good fit for deep cleans and move in / out jobs.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. Tell us how to access your space when you book and we’ll confirm the details with you.
